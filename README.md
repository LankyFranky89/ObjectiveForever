# Objective Forever

A WoW Forever addon that replaces Blizzard's objective tracker.

- **Quests** - your tracked quests, lowest level first, objectives change color as they fill and flash when they progress
- **Professions** - recipes tracked in the profession window, with reagent have/need counts
- **Party Quests** - your party's quests in their own colors (needs [QuestMates](https://github.com/LankyFranky89/QuestMates))
- Click a quest for the map, right-click for the quest log, click the quest icon for the in-world marker, Shift-click to stop tracking
- Stays clear of the right-side action bars, sits under every other window, scrolls with the mouse wheel or the slim scroll bar

## Commands

- `/objf` - turn the tracker on/off (Blizzard's tracker comes back when off)
- `/objf reset` - back to the default spot (Shift-drag the header to move it)
- `/objf scale 1.2` - size
- `/objf debug`, `/objf icons` - diagnostics

`/qmt` works too.
